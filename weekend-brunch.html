The Weekend Experience

Brunch Without the Rush

Weekend brunch is the gentlest way to enjoy dim sum: the pace is slower, the table is bigger, and there is always room for one more basket. Order a few favourites, pour the tea, and let the morning stretch out.

It works just as well for a family lunch as it does for a long catch-up with friends — everyone orders what they like, dishes travel around the table, and the steamer keeps arriving.

  • Relaxed weekend dining
  • Shared dim sum baskets
  • Family & friends gatherings
  • Freshly prepared dishes
  • Warm hospitality at an unhurried pace
