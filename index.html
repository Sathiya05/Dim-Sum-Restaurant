Our Story

A Tradition of Flavor, Made for Sharing.

Dim sum has always been about the table — small baskets passed between friends and family, tea poured again and again, and dishes prepared by hand while you watch the steam rise.

At Golden Steam, our kitchen follows that same spirit: fresh ingredients prepared daily, dumplings folded in small batches, and familiar recipes served with warm hospitality. Nothing is rushed, and everything is meant to be shared.

  • Dumplings folded by hand throughout the day
  • Bamboo steamers instead of shortcuts
  • Tables designed for sharing, not rushing
